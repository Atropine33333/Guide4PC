Given `n` integers, find their minimum, maximum and total.

**Input**: the first line contains an integer `n` (`1 ≤ n ≤ 1000`). The second line contains `n` integers, each with absolute value at most `10^6`.

**Output**: one line with three integers — the minimum, the maximum and the total — separated by single spaces.

### Example

Input:

```text
5
3 -1 4 1 5
```

Output:

```text
-1 5 12
```

### Constraints

- The total may not fit in a 32-bit `int`; choose an appropriate type.
- Do not print trailing spaces.
