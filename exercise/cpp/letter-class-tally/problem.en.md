And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying:

"Number thou the congregation according to their kind, into three camps. Every stroke that riseth up shall go to the camp of the capitals; every stroke that hangeth down, to the camp of the small letters; and every hand that holdeth a figure, to the camp of the digits. And when the numbering is done, bring me the three numbers in order, with one space between them."

So Moses called Aaron and put that word in his hand, saying: "This word is made of letters and digits only, with no space among them, and its length reacheth not beyond a thousand. Number the three camps and bring the numbers; and if a camp be empty, bring a zero."

**In plain words**: given a word made only of English letters and digits, count its uppercase letters, lowercase letters and digits, and print those three counts on one line separated by single spaces.

**Input**: one line holding a word without spaces, of length `1` to `1000`, containing only `A`–`Z`, `a`–`z` and `0`–`9`.

**Output**: one line with three integers — uppercase letters, lowercase letters, digits — separated by single spaces.

### Example

Input:

```text
Hello123
```

Output:

```text
1 4 3
```

### Constraints

- Do not use the locale-dependent character classification functions; compare character ranges directly.
- The output line carries no trailing space.
